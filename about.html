About me

I came to AI through neuroscience. I’m still most interested in the questions that sit between the two, although philosophy, politics and music tend to find their way in too.

Background

I have a First-Class Honours BSc in Neuroscience and I’m now studying an MSc in Computational Neuroscience, Cognition and AI at the University of Nottingham.

I wanted to learn more about how to model the processes I’d been studying in the brain, and what those models might tell us about intelligence more generally.

What’s next?

I’d like to go into research, whether through a PhD or in industry. I’m drawn to work on learning and cognition that brings neuroscience and AI into conversation.

I haven’t settled on one narrow research question yet. At the moment, I’m building my modelling and programming skills, reading around my interests, and working out which questions I could turn into experiments.

Outside the MSc

Guitar, music and politics are also a big part of my interests. Philosophy of mind is another subject I keep finding my way back to, often through questions that start out being about AI.

I use writing to spend a bit longer with those questions. Sometimes I have an argument I want to develop; sometimes I’m just trying to work out what I think.

Contact

I’d be glad to hear from people with overlapping interests, or about opportunities to get involved in research. LinkedIn is the best place to reach me; email works too.
